Qabili Palau

Qabili Palau, or Kabuli Palau, is a beloved Afghan rice dish featuring fragrant spices, tender meat, and perfectly cooked rice, often topped with sweet carrots and raisins, making it a national favorite.

Bolani

Bolani is a stuffed flatbread from the Afghan cuisine. It is usually filled with a mix of potatoes, cilantro, and green peppers, but it can also be filled with pumpkin, red lentils, or chives.

Mantu

Mantu is a traditional Afghan dumpling dish, filled with meat (usually beef or lamb) and steamed, often topped with a garlic yogurt sauce and a sprinkle of dried mint.
